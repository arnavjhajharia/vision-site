# Lucy pricing (final, Arnav, 26 Nov 2026)

**Lucy costs $0–20 a month. Lucy decides each person's price**, including who pays nothing.

- **The call sets the price.** Onboarding opens with a live call with Lucy. In a couple of minutes she gets to know
  you and sets your price anywhere in $0–20, and tells you in plain words why.
- **About 1 in 10 people pay nothing.** That's an outcome of Lucy's decision, kept around 10% overall. It is
  deliberate and fair. Never call it luck, a lottery, a draw or random.
- **Skip the call → flat $20/month** (top of the band). You can take the call any time later and get re-priced.
- **Guardrails:** price never depends on protected traits (race, religion, caste, gender, sexuality, disability,
  age, nationality, etc.); every price comes with an explanation the person understands; anyone can ask to be
  re-priced.
- **Retired, don't use:** Free + Lucy/Vision Max $100, a single $20 plan, the ₹49–199 per-task band.
